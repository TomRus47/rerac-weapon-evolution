# Weapon Evolution 0.3 — ReRAC 0.3.6-alpha

**Draft — not published.** Independent Ratchet & Clank 1 mod for
**ReRAC 0.3.6-alpha**. Gameplay patch **0.1.6.109**, configuration capability
**90**, standalone launcher **0.29.3**. This package updates the previous 0.3
release, which used ReRAC 0.3.4-alpha / patch 0.1.6.59 / launcher 0.17.2.

TomRus47 is the creator of Weapon Evolution only and is not associated with
the development of ReRAC or the original Ratchet & Clank games. No ownership
of the original games, characters, trademarks or assets is claimed. All rights
remain with their respective owners. Huge thanks to the ReRAC developers:
their work on the port makes projects like this mod possible. Existing upstream

## Runtime, progression and New Game Plus

- Move the complete mod onto ReRAC 0.3.6-alpha while retaining the existing
  damage, independent weapon XP, camera-lock and experimental-weapon systems.
  The upstream workspace includes rc-game2; this remains a Ratchet & Clank 1 mod.
- Expand weapon settings and progression to **eight grades**. Ordinary weapons
  reach V5 through XP; their NG+ Gold purchase unlocks V6, followed by V7/V8 XP.
  The OmniWrench has its own XP transitions. Use bright Lime, light blue and
  white V6/V7/V8 icons and matching upgrade effects.
- Rework NG+ Gold displays, models, placement, eligibility, names and prices.
  Require the corresponding V5 weapon; recognize previously bought Gold weapons
  as V6. Keep normal-game Gold displays disabled. Weapons discovery mode removes
  Gold Bolt purchase requirements while preserving normal-bolt prices.
- Add a configurable NG+ difficulty coefficient. Add a kill-chain reward bonus
  that scales normal bolts, weapon XP and Nanotech XP, with a configurable cap
  and an actual multiplier display such as x1.1. Preserve it across travel and
  dialogue; reset it on effective health loss and death. It can also be enabled
  during ordinary Randomizer runs.
- Add optional secret R.Y.N.O. discovery with `hide_ryno`, consistent unlock
  feedback and the corresponding seller restriction. Its location is omitted
  deliberately. Include eligible R.Y.N.O. ammunition in ordinary crate drops.

## Encounters and Nanotech

- Tune enemy HP, damage, movement, perception, chase limits and hostile projectile
  speed/range. Add extra enemies from selected original-HP categories, including
  NG+. Preserve native HP classifications independently of difficulty scaling.
  Unsafe duplication attempts remain pending instead of spawning overlapping or
  invalid actors; native asset and class constraints still apply.
- Add optional health-based enemy damage and independent Nanotech XP progression.
  Premium/Ultra Nanotech add their native +1/+3 capacity separately from the
  configured XP-earned cap. Enemy health changes also affect accepted-damage XP.
- Give bosses attack-specific damage before the existing damage coefficient:
  Blarg station bite 3, Snagglebeast 4; Drek's ordinary attacks/bombs 5, mines
  and final mini-missiles 3, pursuit contact 10.
- Draw expanded Nanotech in two centered rows with the corresponding orb frames
  and proportional spacing. Add optional random 1–4 HP field healing with
  distinct pickup colors, and a bright blue burst on earned health upgrades.
- Exempt specific story projectiles from difficulty speed/range tuning so
  bridge/statue destruction remains functional. Preserve native Hoven turret
  mission speeds only while Ratchet occupies the turret.
- Remove the abandoned Novalis arena experiment. No arena crate or barriers
  are part of this version.

## Discovery modes and Randomizer

- Choose **Off**, **Weapons** or **All** in the launcher. Weapons mode distributes
  the 15 weapons among eligible collectible/custom locations, removes the other
  Gold Bolts and supports early-placement bias and one-weapon-per-planet rules.
- All mode shuffles weapons, gadgets, equipment, Gold Bolts, planetary coordinates
  and supported scripted/shop rewards. It supersedes Weapons mode, retains the
  seed through NG+ and uses a separate save namespace. Runs start on Novalis.
- Include the added locations alongside native checks. Use route requirements,
  accessibility validation, explosive/Gemlik progression safeguards and limited
  Infobot pacing. Old saved layouts are preserved rather than silently rerolled.
- Display rewards with available weapon/gadget/Infobot models, yellow pickup
  feedback and acquisition audio/cutscenes where supported. Use a Gadgetron ammo
  crate for rewards without a dedicated model; real Gold Bolts keep their own
  upright model. Fix the invalid reward rendering that could produce giant
  colored streaks. Support underwater collection without forcing a cutscene or
  immediate weapon equip.
- Keep randomized shop rewards tied to their intended sale locations rather
  than adding every collected weapon as a new sale. Already discovered weapons
  unlock their ammunition. Refuse an empty ammo shop instead of trapping Ratchet
  in its menu, and refresh availability within the current planet.
- Make Thruster-Pack glide and boosted jumps work without requiring an acquired
  Heli-Pack. Keep suction functional at increased enemy movement speed.
- Restrict crate ammunition to discovered weapons. Keep the heavy Visibomb/R.Y.N.O.
  drop rule and its fallback behavior; preserve Ammo-Survival as a separate mode.
- Fix the false initial radial slot in discovery modes and allow skipping the
  Insomniac introduction with Cross/Start. Restore acquisition music after the
  reward jingle rather than interrupting it permanently.

**Randomizer is still in testing and may generate seeds that cannot be
completed.** Automated route checks cannot prove every physical route, collision
or native script. Please report blocked runs and share the affected save as a ZIP;
community reports help improve the generation rules.

The default Randomizer save directory on Windows is:

```text
%APPDATA%\rerac\memcard\randomizer
```

Inside the game's memory-card directory (usually `BASCUS-97199RATCHET`), include
the affected `saveN.bin` and its **complete matching**
`weapon_evolution/saveN.bin/` companion directory, including the `.wev` files.
If a custom save root is selected, use its `randomizer` subfolder instead.
Include the blocked location and missing equipment in the report. The seed
number alone does not describe all saved progression and layout state.

## Weapons, controls and combat feedback

- Add the Pyrocitor Lavagun mode: continuous curved lava streams, configurable
  launch angle, joined visuals and optional triple lanes. Add per-grade
  `lava_damage_bonus_percent`, default **25% extra DPS**, for lava only.
- Add flame-mine-only maximum count and lifetime fields. They override standard
  limits only while flame mode is active, including saved R1 activation. Snapshot
  damage, duration, palette and limits when released; do not rewrite live shots.
- Add per-grade moving wrench throws, visual enemy ignition and a damaging slam
  explosion. Emit one forward, grounded blast per attack, with matching visible
  and damaging radii and normal bomb interactions with nearby objects.
- Give accepted hits consistent red feedback and continuous fire a finite,
  non-stacking burn effect independently of the three-second reaction cooldown.
  Keep accepted damage and XP independent of stun/knockback feedback.
- Permit R2/Circle firing in ordinary movement. Keep L1 first-person view and R1
  crouching with contextual radial-mode handling.
- Add optional saved **D-pad quick selection** for weapons and equippable gadgets.
  Assign with L1 + a D-pad direction on the appropriate pause page. Colored
  borders identify bindings; repeating boots/helmet removes them, while repeating
  a Clank-pack binding cycles owned packs. Respect swimming, magnetic surfaces,
  shops, turrets, cutscenes and native equipment restrictions.
- Add saved radial **R1 Experimental** modes for Pyrocitor, Mine Glove, Visibomb
  and R.Y.N.O., with a per-grade maximum selectable mode. Keep enhancement-only
  options such as triple flames and the second Tesla beam in the launcher.
- Improve backward locked-camera running transitions and maintain ordinary
  movement after changing direction. Fix rapid airborne Walloper reuse and
  retain the earlier native-reaction and projectile-lifetime protections.

## Downloads and updating

The **Windows x64 ZIP** includes the modified `runtime/rerac.exe`, standalone
launcher, shaders, default INI and licenses. Extract the whole archive and open
`ReRAC_Weapon_Evolution.exe`; players do not need Python, Rust or older patches.
Select your own legally extracted game-data folder containing `toc.bin` and
`extract-info.json` if automatic discovery does not find it. No original game
data is included. Do not install this ZIP as an official ReRAC build archive.

The **source ZIP** contains the complete modified workspace, launcher, approved
configuration, tests and documentation from the same commit as the Windows build.
SHA256 sidecars and `release-build.json` identify the assets and source revision.

Back up saves, personal INIs and presets before upgrading. Preserve a personal
profile by copying it deliberately; the fresh package starts from the new default.
Use the newly included runtime if copied launcher settings point to an older EXE.
Save companions use WEVO v9 and read older v1–v8 data; older mod builds cannot be
assumed to read the new format. Randomized layouts already stored in a save are
not regenerated by an update.

This is alpha software built on an alpha runtime. See the repository verification
notes and the attached build report for executed checks and their limits.
